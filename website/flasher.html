USB Web Flasher

Works in Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Connect your device with a data USB cable, choose the board, then press Install.

If the port doesn't show up, hold the boot button (G0) while plugging in. StickC devices may need the CH9102 or CP210x driver.

No board selected

Your browser can't flash over USB. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop. The flasher needs HTTPS or localhost.

Flashing erases the device. Firmware files are loaded from firmware/Viper-<board>.bin on this site.
